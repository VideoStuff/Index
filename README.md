## Details

- **PixelPlanner**: Plans multiscreen video layouts. It works out each screen's pixels, matches pixel density across screens, builds the content canvas, and exports guides and templates for content, playback and switcher teams. It has a to-scale layout editor with stage objects (lecterns, ramps, a person for scale), a Preset Guide, multiple layouts, undo/redo, and save/open as `.json`. Hosted at https://videostuff.github.io/PixelPlanner/.
- **SpeakerTimer**: A single-file timer for talks, panels and events. It supports several simultaneous timers, chained segments, a green/yellow/red stage display, speaker messages, chimes and spoken alerts, scheduling, themes, run-log CSV export and JSON backup. It also has a "ridiculousness dial" (0 to 11) of silly overtime effects.
- **VideoInfo**: A one-page "AV Product Announcements Watch" for November 2026, tracking product news in the AV industry.
